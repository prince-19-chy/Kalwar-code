why we built this

Built in Itahari, for learners who don't always have signal.

Most coding courses assume a fast connection and a laptop. CodeNepal Academy was built for the opposite case: a single phone, patchy data, and a learner who's serious about getting good.

Every lesson downloads once and runs offline after that. Explanations are written in plain, simple English, so technical vocabulary is introduced gradually instead of assumed on day one.

It's built and maintained by a single student developer working out of Itahari, shipping this as a public beta so real learners can shape what gets built next.

installation guide

Get it running in four steps.

Android blocks apps from outside the Play Store by default — here's how to allow this one.

Step 01

Download the APK

Tap the download button below on your Android phone. The file (4 MB) will save to your Downloads folder.

Step 02

Enable "Install unknown apps"

Open the downloaded file. Android will ask for permission the first time — tap Settings, then turn on Allow from this source.

Step 03

Install the app

Go back to the file and tap Install. It takes a few seconds — no need to reopen the browser.

Step 04

Open CodeNepal Academy

Launch the app from your home screen, pick your starting language, and begin lesson one.
